2. What we do not collect

So there is no ambiguity, here is what we do not do:

  • We do not require or offer accounts, registration or sign-in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address or date of birth.
  • We do not access your contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone or files.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone, for any purpose.

3. Information that stays on your device

The app saves some things locally so it is useful between sessions. This stays in the app's private storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us and we cannot read it.

  • Bookmarked and saved topics.
  • Your reading position and which lessons you have opened.
  • Settings such as theme and text size.
  • Cached lesson content, so material you have already opened loads faster and stays readable on a poor connection.

You can erase all of it by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app. We hold no copy to restore.

4. Information our servers receive

Lesson content is delivered from our servers rather than shipped inside the app download. The app therefore makes ordinary network requests, and like every web server, ours keeps access logs. Those logs may contain:

  • the IP address the request came from;
  • the date and time of the request;
  • which content file was requested;
  • basic device and operating system information your device sends with the request;
  • the app version making the request.

We use this only to deliver content, keep the service running, diagnose faults, and protect against abuse such as automated scraping. We do not use it to build a profile of you and we do not try to identify you from it.

Legal basis (GDPR): our legitimate interest in operating and securing the service, Article 6(1)(f).

Retention: server access logs are kept for up to 90 days, then deleted or overwritten.

5. Purchases

Paid apps and in-app purchases are sold and processed by Google Play or Apple, depending on where you downloaded the app. Your payment is made to them, not to us.

We never receive, see or store your card number, bank details or billing address. What we receive is anonymised, aggregated sales reporting: how many purchases happened, in which countries, in which currencies. Nothing in it identifies you.

Our lesson content is hosted with Google Firebase (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited), which acts as our processor and operates the servers described in section 4. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

7. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you, and we keep the correspondence for up to 24 months so we have context if you write again. We do not add you to any mailing list.

Legal basis (GDPR): our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries, Article 6(1)(f).

8. Children

These apps are educational tools intended for university students, self-taught developers and working professionals. They are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email mail@ehsanur.com and we will delete it promptly.

9. Who else is involved

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with data brokers, advertisers or marketing companies.

The only third parties involved are the service providers named in this policy — currently our hosting provider and the app stores that process purchases. They act on our behalf, or as independent controllers for their own operations, under their own published policies.

We may also disclose information where legally required, for example under a valid legal order, or where necessary to investigate abuse of the service or protect the safety of users.

10. Where data is processed

We operate from Bangladesh, and our hosting provider runs data centres in several countries including the United States and the European Union. Information described in section 4 may therefore be processed outside your country.

Where personal data is transferred out of the EEA or the UK, our providers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, or on an adequacy decision where one applies.

11. Your rights

Because we hold no accounts and no contact details, the personal information we hold about any individual is very small. We will still honour every request we can act on.

EU, EEA and UK

You have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time where processing relies on consent. You may also complain to your national data protection authority.

California

Under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA, you have the right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used, to request deletion, to correct inaccurate information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law, and we have not done so in the preceding twelve months. We do not process sensitive personal information.

How to make a request

Email mail@ehsanur.com with the subject line "Privacy request". Tell us which app you use and what you want us to do. We reply within 30 days.

To find server log entries relating to you we generally need the approximate date and time you used the app and the IP address you were using. Without those we may be unable to locate any record, and we will tell you so honestly rather than guess.

Deleting your data

The data held on your own device needs no involvement from us — clear the app's storage or uninstall it, as described in section 3. For anything else, see our data deletion page.

12. Security

Content is delivered over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to our hosting console is protected by strong, unique credentials and two-factor authentication. The most effective protection is structural: we collect no accounts, contact details or payment information, so there is very little about you for anyone to take from us.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date and version at the top. For changes that materially affect your privacy — for example if we begin showing advertisements or add analytics — we will give notice inside the app before the change takes effect, and where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent.

We will not apply a materially different practice to information already collected without telling you first.
