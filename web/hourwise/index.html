Questions

The ones worth asking first.

Can it read my documents or emails?

No. It records which application was in front and for how long. It cannot open, read or index the contents of anything — not a document, not an email, not a message. It takes no screenshots and does not read your keystrokes or your clipboard.

Why does a block say "Worked in Microsoft Word" rather than the file name?

Because that is what an app distributed through the Mac App Store is permitted to see. Reading another app's window title needs Accessibility access, which Apple's sandbox rules do not allow. So Hourwise records the app and the time honestly rather than guessing, and your rules, your corrections and your calendar do the rest.

Do I have to start and stop a timer?

No, and that is the point. Hourwise runs in the menu bar and keeps the record while you work. There is nothing to start, so there is nothing to forget.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Nothing. Everything you have recorded stays on your Mac and stays readable, and tracking and review keep working. What you lose is the Pro half — generating new invoices, exporting, and looking further back than seven days. Invoices you already made are yours regardless.

How do I cancel?

In your Mac's App Store settings, under your subscriptions. Apple handles billing, so there is no account with us to close and nobody to call. Cancel any time and Pro stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.

I already subscribe. What if I buy Lifetime?

You get Pro for good, but the App Store can't cancel your subscription for you. Cancel it yourself in your App Store settings, or you'll be charged for both. The app warns you about this before you buy.

Can I get my data out?

Yes, always. One button writes every time entry you have ever recorded to a CSV, and another backs up the whole database to a single file you can keep anywhere.
